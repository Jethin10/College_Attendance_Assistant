How this works

  • Checks every subject. NIET's current published policy sets a 75% target in each theory and practical subject.
  • Also shows your overall figure. It is calculated from the same present and total counts shown by the ERP, so you can cross-check the projection.
  • Reads your own portal session. Attendance and your dated timetable are read from the ERP while you are signed in. No passwords are stored.
  • Stays on this device. Everything is kept in local browser storage. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no analytics.
  • Estimates, not a guarantee. Being below 75% does not let this extension decide exam eligibility. Always confirm with NIET and the ERP; approved OD, condonation, and exceptions are not counted.
